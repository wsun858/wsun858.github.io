I am a PhD student in Computer Science at the University of Wisconsin-Madison, where I have worked with Michael Gleicher and Yin Li. I received my BS and MS degrees from UC San Diego, where I worked with Pamela Cosman and Xinyu Zhang.

My research is in robotics and computer vision. I currently work on world models for soft robots. Previously, I was a research intern at Nokia Bell Labs, where I worked on multi-agent LLM systems with Thomas Woo.

In summer 2026, I will be joining Sigma Computing as a research intern, working with Marwan Mattar.
